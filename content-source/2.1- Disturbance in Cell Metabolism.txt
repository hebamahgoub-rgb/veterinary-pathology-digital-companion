        <h2>Start Here: Principles of Cellular Accumulation</h2>
        <ul>
          <li><strong>Homeostatic Balance:</strong> Normal cells maintain a precise equilibrium among substrate uptake, synthesis, utilization, transport, and removal.</li>
          <li><strong>Mechanism of Accumulation:</strong> Any disturbance in this metabolic sequence causes intracellular or extracellular storage of organic or inorganic material.</li>
          <li><strong>Reversibility vs. Injury:</strong> Mild or transient metabolic alterations may be completely reversible, whereas severe or persistent deposits induce permanent structural and functional injury.</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
